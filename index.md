# eBayListingAssistant Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 2, 2026**

## Purpose

eBayListingAssistant is a private seller-management application used to help its operator prepare and manage eBay listings and related seller functions.

## Information the application processes

The application may process information supplied by its operator, including listing descriptions, item photographs, item attributes, pricing and shipping selections, and other information needed to manage listings.

The application uses eBay OAuth authorization to access permitted seller-account functions. It does not collect or store the operator's eBay password.

When necessary to perform authorized seller functions, eBay may provide account, listing, transaction, order, shipping, or other information. The application does not use such information for advertising, unrelated profiling, or sale to third parties.

## How information is used

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, audit, and improve the operator's eBay selling workflow and to perform seller actions explicitly authorized through the application.

## Storage and security

Application state is primarily maintained in the operator-controlled eBayListingAssistant environment. OAuth credentials and tokens are treated as secrets and are not intended to be exposed in listing packages, public pages, logs, or diagnostic output.

Google Drive may be used as a transport mechanism for operator-created listing packages and result artifacts.

## Sharing

eBayListingAssistant does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to eBay as necessary to perform authorized seller functions and to infrastructure/services deliberately used by the operator to run the workflow, such as Google Drive for listing-package transport.

## Buyer and other eBay-user information

The application is seller-focused and does not collect buyer information for independent marketing or profiling. If eBay provides information about buyers or other eBay users as necessary for authorized seller functions, that information will be used only for those functions and handled in accordance with applicable eBay requirements.

## Retention and deletion

Information is retained only for legitimate operational, transaction, troubleshooting, audit, security, or compliance needs. Data that is no longer needed should be deleted or anonymized when required by applicable eBay requirements or law.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when application functionality or applicable requirements change. The published version will identify its effective date.
